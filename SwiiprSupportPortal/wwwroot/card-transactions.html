Card Transactions Export

Fetch card transactions from the Swiipr platform and export them as CSV.

Bearer token (optional)

Paste a JWT to use it instead of signing in. It is kept in this tab's sessionStorage for the session window (survives refresh and page navigation, cleared when the tab closes, on expiry or on Clear), never logged, and sent only to the local proxy.

1. Card tokens

One token per line (any length; tabs, commas and quotes from Excel are fine). Paste, or upload a .txt/.csv file. Tokens are trimmed and de-duplicated.
